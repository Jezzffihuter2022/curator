- For the 1stDibs Trifari pieces, ask the seller for a photo of the mark. A crown-over-T "Crown Trifari" mark dates to c.1940s-1950s. A plain "TRIFARI ©" or "TRIFARI TM" fits the 1970s-80s dating these sellers give. That makes them vintage but late, and less collectible than Alfred Philippe-era pieces.
