# Logistics, Import Tax and Landed Cost: one vintage costume-jewelry brooch imported into mainland China (US / UK-EU / Japan), with item-price ceilings for a total of CNY 2,000 or less

Research date: 2026-11-23. FX is the CFETS central parity for 2026-11-19. Where a figure is older than 2025, or comes from a secondary or anecdotal source, it is flagged as such.

**Important framing correction for the report writer:** The brief refers to the 《中华人民共和国进境物品进口税率表》 and "行邮税". Since **2024-12-01** these have been superseded by the 《进境物品关税、增值税、消费税征收办法》 (税委会公告2024年第11号), issued under the new 《关税法》, together with 海关总署公告2024年第175号. That announcement publishes a new 《进境物品分类表》 and 《计税价格表》, which use "综合税率" (composite rates). The practical rate tiers for jewelry (13% / 20% / 50%) did not change, but the governing documents, the terminology and the parcel value limit (now CNY 2,000) did. People still say "行邮税" colloquially.

## Q1. China's personal-article import tax: current rates, jewelry classification, the CNY 50 exemption, dutiable value, postal vs. courier vs. cross-border e-commerce

### Takeaway
Under the rules in force since 2024-12-01, a personal-use mailed brooch is taxed at **tax = 计税价格 × 综合税率**. The rate depends on how customs classifies the piece:
- **20%** for "其他首饰" (costume or fashion jewelry, including gold-plated base metal)
- **13%** for "金银" (precious-metal jewelry, e.g. sterling silver)
- **50%** for "贵重首饰及珠宝玉石", which also expressly includes base-metal *clad* (包贵金属) jewelry without diamonds

If the calculated tax is CNY 50 or less, the parcel is released tax-free. Mailed parcels qualify for this simplified collection only if their total value is CNY 2,000 or less, or if they are one indivisible item. Commercial couriers may instead clear a parcel as goods (C类), which means normal duty + 13% VAT.

### Cited Findings
**Legal basis and computation**
- The 《进境物品关税、增值税、消费税征收办法》 was published by 税委会公告2024年第11号 on 2024-11-29 under 《关税法》 Art. 5 and took effect 2024-12-01. — [MOFCOM repost of 税委会公告2024年第11号](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html); [gov.cn cover page](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202411/content_6990193.htm); [MOF 关税司](http://gss.mof.gov.cn/gzdt/zhengcefabu/202411/t20241129_3948540.htm)
- **Art. 6:** Items that customs confirms as "个人合理自用" are taxed under the simplified method, which combines duty, VAT and consumption tax into one levy. For mailed items the method covers "总值两千元人民币以内的寄递物品，或者总值超过两千元人民币的不可分割单件寄递物品". — [MOFCOM repost](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- **Art. 7:** "应纳税额按照计税价格乘以综合税率计算。" — [same](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- **Art. 9:** Tax is computed in CNY. Foreign-currency prices are converted at the 计征汇率 (customs collection rate) on the declaration date. — [same](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- **Art. 13, the CNY 50 exemption:** "应征税额在五十元人民币以内的寄递物品，海关予以免税放行。" The threshold is on the **tax amount**, not on the item's value. — [same](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- **Art. 15:** Items beyond reasonable personal quantities, along with some other categories, are taxed as imported goods. — [same](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- The mailed-item value limit was raised from CNY 1,000 to CNY 2,000 from 2024-12-01 (海关总署公告2024年第176号). An indivisible single item is not bound by the amount limit. — [DHL China regulatory changes (secondary)](https://www.dhl.com/discover/zh-tw/ship-with-dhl/export-with-dhl/china-regulatory-changes); [JETRO Q&A (secondary)](https://www.jetro.go.jp/world/qa/04A-051022.html)

**Classification table (海关总署公告2024年第175号, 附件1 《进境物品分类表》, category 08000000 "金银、首饰及珠宝玉石"; text taken from the HK TID reproduction of the customs.gov.cn notice)**
- 贵重首饰及珠宝玉石（不含钻石）, **50%**: "包括天然或养殖珍珠、宝石或半宝石（不含钻石），用天然或养殖珍珠、宝石或半宝石（不含钻石）制成的物品，以贱金属为底的非镶嵌钻石的包贵金属首饰。"
- 钻石及钻石首饰, **20%**.
- 金银, **13%**: "包括金、银、铂等贵金属及包贵金属，贵金属及包贵金属制的首饰（以贱金属为底的非镶嵌钻石的包贵金属首饰除外）、金银器和其他制品。"
- 其他首饰, **20%**: "其他不能归入上述类别的首饰。"
- The 《计税价格表》 lists 08010000 金银 (13%), 08020100 钻石及钻石首饰 (20%), 08020200 贵重首饰及珠宝玉石 (50%) and 08990000 其他首饰 (20%). The unit is 件 and the 计税价格 for all of them is "另行确定", meaning there is **no fixed table value for jewelry**.
- Category 20000000 邮票、艺术品、收藏品 is taxed at 20%, including 20020000 艺术品、收藏品.
- Catch-all 25000000 其他物品 is taxed at 20%.
- Overall tiers in the table are 3% (some drugs), 13%, 20% and 50%.

  Source for all of the above: [HK TID reproduction of 海关总署公告2024年第175号 with annexes (PDF)](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2024/ci2024985a.pdf), which links the original at [customs.gov.cn](http://www.customs.gov.cn/customs/302249/2480148/6233507/index.html).
- The older pre-2024 归类表 (PDF created in 2021) had the same jewelry split: 50% / 20% / 13% with the same "以贱金属为底的非镶嵌钻石的包贵金属首饰" wording. Its category was headed "金银、贵重首饰及珠宝玉石" and its catch-all was 27000000 其他物品 at 20%. **This is the older version, superseded.** — [ECMS-hosted 进境物品归类表 PDF](https://www.ecmsglobal.com/resources/zh-cn/%E8%BF%9B%E5%A2%83%E7%89%A9%E5%93%81%E5%BD%92%E7%B1%BB%E8%A1%A8.pdf)

**Dutiable value (计税价格), 海关总署公告2024年第175号 Art. 3**
- "进境物品的计税价格以实际购买价格为基础确定". Customs may require purchase vouchers before release.
- If the price is doubtful or absent, customs uses the 《计税价格表》 price, unless the item is worth 2x or more, or half or less, of that price. Failing that, it uses "其他合理方法", with preference for "相同物品相同来源地最近时间的市场零售价格".
- The announcement repeals 海关总署公告2012年第15号, 2016年第25号, 2018年第140号 and 2019年第63号.

  Source: [MOFCOM repost of 175号](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_96b7d864073d438798f345236a52fcc2.html); [HK TID PDF](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2024/ci2024985a.pdf)

**No 2026 change found**
- The 2026 tariff adjustment (税委会公告2025年第11号, effective 2026-01-01) covers goods tariffs: 935 provisional rates and 8,972 tariff lines. Searches found no 2026 announcement changing the 进境物品 composite rates. — [Xinhua, 2025-12-29](https://www.news.cn/politics/20251229/61b31e3832e64131abb1bfc4c97cbfd7/c.html)

**Commercial couriers (DHL / FedEx / UPS)**
- **B类 快件** means "境内收寄件人（自然人）收取或者交寄的个人自用物品". **C类** means low-value goods of CNY 5,000 or less (excluding freight, insurance and other charges) that do not involve licences, export rebates or FX settlement. **D类** covers goods over CNY 5,000, which follow general-trade formal declaration. The FedEx document is dated April 2025. — [FedEx China tariff guide PDF](https://fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/apac-asia-pacific/downloads/fedex-china-us-tariff-zh-cn.pdf); [DHL Category C page](https://www.dhl.com/discover/zh-tw/ship-with-dhl/export-with-dhl/china-category-c)
- DHL advises declaring the correct item name and value and keeping payment proof. The recipient normally pays duties, which DHL advances and then collects, and DHL releases the parcel only once it has been reimbursed. — [DHL duties FAQ](https://mydhl.express.dhl/kh/zh/help-and-support/faqs/duties-and-taxes.html) (secondary summary)
- Shippo (April 2026) describes courier-cleared US→China goods as HS-based duty + 13% VAT, plus about 10% retaliatory tariff on US-origin goods "under a truce extended through November 10, 2026". — [Shippo blog](https://goshippo.com/blog/shipping-to-china-from-the-us-rules-costs-options) (secondary; tariff status after 2026-11-10 not verified)
- 税委会公告2025年第9号 stopped part of the US additional-tariff measures from 2025-11-10, and 2025年第10号 adjusted them. The full texts were not retrieved. — [HK TID circular (PDF)](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2025/ci9652025a.pdf)

**Cross-border e-commerce retail import (跨境电商零售进口, e.g. 天猫国际 / 京东国际), 财关税〔2018〕49号, from 2019-01-01**
- Single-transaction limit CNY 5,000 and annual limit CNY 26,000. — [chinatax.gov.cn](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5202372/content.html)
- Within the limits, duty is 0%, and VAT and consumption tax are charged at 70% of the statutory amount with no exemption threshold. — [新京报](https://www.bjnews.com.cn/detail/154358631114920.html); formula: tax = 完税价格 × [(VAT + CT)/(1 − CT)] × 70% — [amz123 (secondary)](https://www.amz123.com/ask/DIIPRtZS)
- A single-item order over CNY 5,000 but within the annual limit pays the full goods rates. — [chinatax.gov.cn](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5202372/content.html)

### Inferences
- **Costume jewelry (electroplated gold on base metal, enamel, glass rhinestones) most likely falls under "其他首饰", 20%.** The 50% line names *包贵金属* (precious-metal-*clad*) base-metal jewelry. In HS/Chinese tariff usage, "包贵金属" (clad, i.e. a mechanically bonded layer such as rolled gold or gold-filled) is distinct from electroplating (镀金). Electroplated pieces are normally "仿首饰" (imitation jewellery, HS 7117), not clad. This reading is my own inference from general HS conventions and was **not confirmed by a customs ruling**.
- **"Gold-filled" or "rolled gold" vintage pieces (common in US and UK vintage, marked "1/20 12K GF" or "RG") could literally fit the 50% "以贱金属为底的…包贵金属首饰" wording.** Advise buyers to treat those as a 50% risk.
- **Glass rhinestones are not "宝石或半宝石"**, so they should not by themselves trigger the 50% line. If customs believes the stones are real (for example the listing says "crystal" or "stone"), mis-classification is possible.
- **A vintage brooch could arguably be declared as "收藏品" (20020000).** The rate is the same 20%, so it makes no difference for costume pieces.
- **Because jewelry's table value is "另行确定", customs relies on the actual purchase price.** Keep the invoice or PayPal and platform receipt.
- **Whether shipping, buyer's premium and proxy fees enter the 计税价格 is not spelled out.** The text says "实际购买价格". The model therefore taxes item + buyer's premium (part of what the buyer pays the seller or platform) and shows a sensitivity case where shipping is also taxed.
- **The CNY 50 exemption effectively means items with 计税价格 of CNY 250 or less (at 20%) or CNY 384.6 or less (at 13%) are tax-free if inspected.** At 50% the cut-off is CNY 100. This is irrelevant for the CNY 1,000–1,600 items in the ceilings below.
- **Postal parcels (EMS / China Post) are cleared as 物品 and, in practice, not every parcel is assessed (sampling).** This is anecdotal and widely reported; no source was retrieved. The model conservatively assumes the parcel **is** taxed.
- **Commercial couriers shipped by a business seller are likely cleared as C类 goods** at MFN duty + 13% VAT (+ possible US-origin additional tariff), unless the recipient declares B类 personal items with ID. That can cost more than the 20% composite rate, and couriers add disbursement or brokerage fees.
- **CBEC example (inference from the formula):** costume jewelry with no consumption tax would be taxed at 13% × 70% = **9.1%**. If an item were treated as 贵重首饰 with 10% import consumption tax, the rate would be (13% + 10%)/(1 − 10%) × 70% ≈ 17.9%. The 10% consumption-tax rate for 贵重首饰 is from my background knowledge and **not verified** in this session.
- **CBEC is mostly irrelevant for one-off vintage pieces,** which are rarely sold through bonded or CBEC channels, but it is the cheapest tax route if one is available.

### Gaps
- I could not open customs.gov.cn directly. The rates come from the HK TID reproduction of the customs.gov.cn notice, which is an official HK government reproduction of the GACC text.
- I found no official customs ruling on electroplated vs. clad brooches, and no reliable 知乎 or 什么值得买 accounts specific to jewelry tax. Searches returned DHL pages only.
- The MFN duty for HS 7117 (imitation jewellery) and 7113 (silver jewellery) in the 2026 tariff was not retrieved, so the courier "as goods" cost cannot be computed precisely.
- Whether US additional tariffs apply to personal-article (物品) clearance is not established, and the status of the US–China truce after 2026-11-10 is unknown.
- I found no source on whether shipping is included in 计税价格 for mailed items in practice.

## Q2. Does sterling silver change the classification or rate?

### Takeaway
Yes. Gold-plated **sterling silver** jewelry is "贵金属…制的首饰", which falls in **金银, 13%**, lower than the 20% for base-metal costume jewelry. The 50% line is aimed at natural or cultured pearls, gem or semi-precious stones, and base-metal *clad* jewelry, not at silver.

### Cited Findings
- The 金银 13% scope is quoted in full: "金、银、铂等贵金属及包贵金属，贵金属及包贵金属制的首饰（以贱金属为底的非镶嵌钻石的包贵金属首饰除外）、金银器和其他制品". — [HK TID reproduction of 175号 annex](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2024/ci2024985a.pdf)
- The 贵重首饰及珠宝玉石 50% scope (pearls, gem or semi-precious stones and articles made of them, and base-metal clad jewelry) and the 其他首饰 20% scope are both given in Q1. — [same](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2024/ci2024985a.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Sterling (925) with enamel and glass rhinestones should stay at 13%.** Silver is the base material, and the 50% stone clause applies to genuine gem or semi-precious stones.
- **To claim 13%, the declaration and invoice should say "sterling silver / 925 银镀金胸针".** A vague "brooch" or "jewelry" may default to 其他首饰 at 20%. This is my inference about clearance practice, not a sourced fact.
- **The 13% vs. 20% difference on a CNY 1,000 item is about CNY 70.** It moves the price ceiling by only about 5–7%.

### Gaps
- I found no customs guidance on mixed-material pieces, for example a silver brooch with a real marcasite or pearl element, which could tip it into 50%.

## Q3. Shipping options and typical costs for a small (under 200 g) parcel

### Takeaway
For a sub-200 g brooch the cheapest tracked postal routes are:
- **Japan:** Japan Post Small Packet airmail ¥450 at 200 g (untracked unless registered), or EMS ¥1,450 up to 500 g
- **US:** USPS First-Class Package International, about USD 30 for 1 lb (third-party quote, April 2026)
- **UK:** Royal Mail International Tracked to China, about £11–£18 depending on format and weight band

Express couriers cost USD 65 or more and raise the risk of goods-style clearance. eBay International Shipping lists China as eligible, but some sellers report China being auto-excluded.

### Inferences
- **A brooch in a padded box usually weighs 50–150 g.** The 1 lb USPS figure is therefore an upper bound, and seller-calculated FCPIS for about 4 oz is likely somewhat lower (not verified).
- **Proxy services (Buyee and others) usually ship from their own warehouse** using their own menu of EMS, airmail, or their own courier and consolidated lines. The Japan Post retail rates above are the floor, and proxies may add handling (see Q5).
- **The postal channel (USPS → China Post / EMS, Royal Mail → China Post, Japan Post EMS) is preferable for this use case,** because it clears as 物品 under the 20% / 13% composite rate.

### Gaps
- **No verified EU → China small-parcel prices** (Deutsche Post, PostNL, La Poste) were retrieved. The Catawiki example uses an assumed €25 seller shipping charge, which must be flagged as an assumption.
- I found no official USPS Notice 123 price for 4–8 oz to China.
- I found no current eBay International Shipping China rate examples.
- I found no US forwarder (Planet Express, MyUS, etc.) fee data.
- Royal Mail weight bands for the China prices are unverified.
- Japan Post's registered-mail fee and ePacket availability to China were not checked.

### Inferences
- **The model uses a 1.5% FX/card fee on all foreign-currency payments.** Use 0% for a fee-waiving 全币种 card, and about 3–4% if paying via PayPal's own conversion. Tax paid in CNY at clearance carries no FX fee.
- **Card networks' own exchange rates differ slightly from the CFETS mid-rate.** This is typically small (within about 0.5%) but not verified here.

### Gaps

- Bank of China, ICBC and similar card fee schedules were not retrieved.

## Q6. Landed-cost model and worked examples

### Takeaway
**Model** (all in CNY at CFETS 2026-11-19):

> **Total = (P·(1+b) + Fp + S) × (1 + f) + t × (P·(1+b) + Fp)**

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| P | item price |
| b | buyer's-premium percentage |
| Fp | fixed premium parts (e.g. Catawiki €3) |
| S | shipping plus proxy fees |
| f | FX/card fee, 1.5% |
| t | composite rate: 20% costume, 13% sterling, 50% worst case |

Tax is set to 0 when it is CNY 50 or less.

At 20% (base case), the totals are:
- **USD 150 eBay item + FCPIS:** CNY 1,427
- **JPY 20,000 Mercari via Buyee + EMS:** CNY 1,125
- **EUR 120 Catawiki lot (assumed €25 shipping):** CNY 1,380
- **GBP 100 UK eBay item + Royal Mail Tracked:** CNY 1,187

### Cited Findings (inputs)
- FX rates. — [Xinhua 2026-11-19](https://www.xinhuanet.com/fortune/20261119/a4b5c83840da4213ae17baee803f7515/c.html)
- Composite rates of 13%, 20% and 50%. — [175号 annex via HK TID](https://www.tid.gov.hk/en/tradecircular/files/2024/ci2024985a.pdf)
- CNY 50 exemption and the Art. 7 formula. — [征收办法](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/zgdwjjmywg/art/2025/art_1e3054b72cf8471abeed5b224eda0c8d.html)
- USPS FCPIS $29.90 and PMI $63.09. — [Shippo](https://goshippo.com/blog/shipping-to-china-from-the-us-rules-costs-options)
- Japan Post EMS ¥1,450 and small packet ¥750 / ¥450. — [Japan Post EMS](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/list-ems/zone1_en.html); [Japan Post small packet](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/list-normal/zone1_en.html)
- Royal Mail £13.15 (band assumed). — [Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/sending/international/international-tracked)
- Catawiki 9% + €3. — [Catawiki](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/fees/what-is-the-buyer-protection-fee)
- Buyee ¥500 service fee and ¥500 plan. — [kakuni-lab](https://japanproxy.kakuni-lab.com/buyee-fees)

### Inferences
- **On these routes, tax is the largest add-on after the item price,** at about 14–17% of the total in the 20% case. Shipping ranges from about 9% of the total (Japan EMS) to about 26% (US PMI).

- **Proxy and courier routes can add costs not modelled here:** courier disbursement or brokerage fees, forwarder receiving and repacking fees, and US-origin additional tariffs on goods-type clearance.
- **A search summary attributed a "2% of advanced duty" service fee to DHL.** It was not verified, so it is not used.

### Gaps
- The EU shipping figure is assumed.
- eBay International Shipping's actual China shipping and import-charge quotes were not obtained. eBay may compute import charges on a goods basis rather than at the 物品 composite rate.
- US forwarder fees are unknown.

### Inferences
