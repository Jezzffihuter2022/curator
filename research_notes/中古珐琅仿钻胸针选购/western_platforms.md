# Western Online Marketplaces for Vintage Signed Enamel + Rhinestone Brooches (shipping to mainland China, landed budget ≤ CNY 2,000)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Several platform pages (eBay sold search, Ruby Lane search) returned 403/503 to automated fetch, so live sold-price samples are thin. Every figure below has a source; anything I could not check is listed under Gaps. FX note: the CNY conversions are my approximations at about 7.1 CNY/USD, not a sourced rate. Check the live rate before use.

## eBay (US/UK/DE/FR): shipping to China, price ranges, sold filters, search terms, buyer protection

### Takeaway
eBay is the most practical platform. Mainland China is on the official eBay International Shipping (EIS) destination list for US sellers, and eBay's buyer help page says the Money Back Guarantee covers international purchases. eBay's China-specific HDB route excludes second-hand, auction and Best Offer items, so it is not usable for vintage brooches. Signed enamel + rhinestone brooches from mid-tier makers commonly sell at about USD 40–200. That leaves room in a CNY 2,000 budget for one good piece or two modest ones once shipping is included.

### Cited Findings
- **EIS covers mainland China.** The official eBay International Shipping eligible-destination table lists "China (CN)" separately from Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. — [eBay Help: eBay International Shipping program](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348)
- **Who arranges EIS.** EIS is a program for eligible US sellers, so the seller has to enable it. The list "is subject to change." — [eBay Help id=5348](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348); [pages.ebay.ca international-shipping](https://pages.ebay.ca/international-shipping)
- **EIS seller protection.** Import fees "may be collected from the buyer at checkout or on delivery." Sellers are protected from Money Back Guarantee cases for items shipped through EIS, meaning eBay, not the seller, absorbs transit problems. — [eBay Help id=5348](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348)
- **Buyer-side rules (eBay help for international buyers):**
  - "International purchases are covered" by the Money Back Guarantee if other eligibility requirements are met.
  - EIS buyers pay shipping at checkout and can choose to pay import charges at checkout or on delivery. Tracking is included, and returns are accepted within 30 days.
  - UK sellers use the Global Shipping Programme, which is prepaid with customs clearance included.
  - With other services, duties may be due on arrival.
  — [eBay Help: International purchases and shipping (id=4057)](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/shipping-delivery/changing-delivery-address-method/international-purchases-shipping?id=4057)
- **HDB International Shipping Services (China only).** It is offered to buyers with a China address. Conditions:
  - Listing price under CNY 2,000 and weight 5 kg or less.
  - **Auction, Best Offer and second-hand items are not eligible.**
  - Requires the recipient's real-name ID and ID photos.
  - eBay Money Back Guarantee "no longer applies once HDB begins international shipping." Claims go to HDB instead.
  - Reference rates (up to 1 kg, from the Oregon warehouse): USD 10 for items valued USD 0–34.99, USD 18.40 for USD 100–109.99, USD 29.90 for USD 200–209.99, USD 40.25 for USD 290–299.99.
  — [eBay: HDB international shipping to China](https://www.ebay.com/cdp/help/freight/hdb)
- **Seller exclusions.** A user post on the eBay community says China appeared as eligible on some listings but not others. Treat this as anecdotal. — [eBay Community](https://community.ebay.com/t5/user/viewprofilepage/user-id/10247215)
- **Example prices (asking or sold; see notes):**
  - Signed Boucher green/blue rhinestone peacock brooch: listed at USD 180 or Best Offer. The listing had ended and the sale outcome was not visible. — [eBay itm 184190829009](https://www.ebay.com/itm/184190829009)
  - Crown Trifari green enamel + rhinestone feather brooch: reported sold for USD 105, marked down from USD 150. Year not confirmed. — [eBay itm (via search snippet)](https://ebay.com/itm/267070586957); [tryrelic Trifari guide](https://www.tryrelic.app/antiques/trifari-rhinestone-brooch)
  - Yellow enamel Trifari flower brooch: about USD 40–50 in recent sales, per an aggregator value guide (secondary source). — [tryrelic: Trifari Enamel Flower Brooch](https://www.tryrelic.app/antiques/trifari-enamel-flower-brooch)
  - Gold-tone rhinestone Trifari and Coro: current eBay/Etsy asking prices mostly USD 12–25 (aggregator; asking, not sold). — [tryrelic](https://www.tryrelic.app/antiques/trifari-rhinestone-brooch)
  - Signed Trifari, Haskell, Eisenberg, Weiss and Coro pieces: USD 50–800. Coro Duettes: USD 35–550+. Missing stones and lost enamel "sharply reduce value." (Aggregator guide.) — [Estimonia vintage costume jewelry value guide](https://estimonia.com/guides/jewelry/vintage-costume-jewelry-value-guide)
- **Exact sold data.** WorthPoint holds sold-price history behind a login. Its public snippets did not show prices. — [WorthPoint Worthopedia example](https://novaqa.sandbox.worthpoint.com/worthopedia/signed-boucher-vintage-ruby-red-glass-1981890850)
- **Mark dating aid for Boucher.** After 1955 a copyright symbol appears before the name, and later pieces are marked "Marcel Boucher" or "Boucher." Do not confuse Boucher with Boucheron, which is fine jewelry. — search-result summary from [1stDibs Boucher pages](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/vintage-boucher-brooches) and [Aguttes lot (Boucheron)](https://www.aguttes.com/en/lot/142769/23773441-boucheron-broche-barette-perles-supposees-fines-diamants)
- **cn.ebay.com.** eBay runs a Chinese-language browse site with brooch category pages. Some listings there show "free international shipping," others about CNY 35 shipping. — [cn.ebay.com brooch category example](https://cn.ebay.com/b/Joan-Rivers-Crystal-Enamel-Fashion-Brooches-Pins/50677/bn_77223422)

### Inferences
- **Preferred route.** Buy from US sellers whose listing shows EIS shipping to China. This keeps the eBay Money Back Guarantee, puts import charges at checkout, and avoids a forwarder.
- **Avoid HDB for this category.** Vintage brooches are second-hand, and many are auctions or Best Offer listings, which HDB excludes. HDB also ends eBay protection once it ships.
- **Budget fit.** About USD 150–200 for the item plus USD 25–45 for EIS shipping and import charges roughly matches CNY 2,000. Typical prices are Trifari / Coro / BSK-tier enamel at USD 40–150 and Boucher figurals at USD 100–250+. Rare Boucher figurals, Mazer or Trifari sterling "jelly bellies," and early Eisenberg sterling will often exceed the budget.
- **Searching on eBay:**
  - Turn on "Sold items" (and "Completed items") in the left filter panel, or add `&LH_Sold=1&LH_Complete=1` to the URL, to see actual sale prices from roughly the last 90 days.
  - Set "Item location" (US only, UK, EU) and "Ships to: China."
  - Useful terms: "Boucher enamel brooch signed", "Trifari sterling enamel", "Coro Craft sterling", "Mazer sterling brooch", "Hattie Carnegie enamel pin", "Eisenberg Original sterling", "Christian Dior Germany brooch" / "Grosse Dior", "YSL brooch vintage", "Givenchy brooch vintage", "Butler & Wilson brooch".
  - Add "vermeil" or "sterling" to find 1940s pieces, and "figural" to find animal or flower pieces.
  - On ebay.de / ebay.fr, use local words: "Brosche Emaille Strass signiert", "broche émail strass signée".
  - (Filter mechanics come from general eBay UI knowledge. The help-page fetch for sold filters was not retrieved.)
- **UK sellers on GSP.** China's coverage under the Global Shipping Programme was not confirmed (see Gaps).

### Gaps
- No live sample of 20–30 sold listings could be retrieved: eBay returned 403 for sold-search pages. Current median sold prices per maker remain unverified.
- I could not confirm whether the UK Global Shipping Programme / eBay International Standard Delivery covers mainland China, or whether ebay.de and ebay.fr sellers can reach China through an eBay-managed program.
- EIS shipping cost to China for a sub-100 g parcel was not found.
- How China's personal-parcel (行邮) rules apply to EIS-collected import charges was not verified.

## Etsy: shipping to China, typical vintage dealer prices, review reliability

### Takeaway
Etsy has deep, browsable vintage costume-jewelry stock. Whether an item can be sent to China is set by each seller, and many US sellers block international shipping or quote high rates. When a seller does not ship to China, a US forwarder is the usual workaround. I found no official Etsy statement on vintage items shipped to China.

### Cited Findings
- **Seller-set international shipping.** Sellers can opt out of shipping abroad. "Not all Etsy sellers offer shipping to China." Buyers commonly see blocked international addresses or very expensive shipping (forwarder marketing source). — [GlobalShopaholics: Buy from Etsy and ship to China](https://globalshopaholics.com/en-cn/blog/buy-from-etsy-and-ship-to-china/)
- **Checkout problems for China addresses.** An Etsy community thread reports that a buyer in China could not purchase even though the seller's shipping settings were correct, which suggests technical friction. — [Etsy Community thread](https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Shipping-Settings-are-Correct-but-Buyer-in-China-Can-t-Purchase/m-p/147213555)
- **Customs.** Etsy says buyers may have to pay customs fees and tariffs on international packages. — [Etsy Help: Who pays for customs](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015691007-Who-Pays-For-Customs-Fees-)
- **Country-of-origin labels.** Non-US sellers report that Etsy labels default the country of origin to the seller's home country, which creates problems for vintage items. — [Value Added Resource](https://valueaddedresource.net/etsy-tariff-turmoil-country-of-origin-confusion)
- **Price level.** Gold-tone rhinestone Trifari and Coro asking prices on eBay and Etsy are mostly USD 12–25 (aggregator). — [tryrelic](https://www.tryrelic.app/antiques/trifari-rhinestone-brooch)

### Inferences
- Etsy asking prices for curated vintage dealers usually run above eBay sold prices. Use eBay sold data to benchmark any Etsy offer. This is an inference; no Etsy price study was found.
- **Reviews.** Etsy reviews are per shop, not per item. Shops with many reviews that mention "as described" and "well packed" are a reasonable proxy for reliability. Reviews do not authenticate makers' marks, so ask for clear photos of the mark.

### Gaps
- No verified Etsy policy page on shipping to mainland China, and no official review-integrity data.
- No sampled Etsy listing prices for the named makers. Etsy search pages were not fetched.

## Ruby Lane, 1stDibs, Chairish: curated dealers, price premiums, items under ~USD 250, international shipping

### Takeaway
- **1stDibs:** a price premium for designer vintage. Boucher enamel brooches there average in the thousands of USD.
- **Chairish:** officially facilitates shipping only within the US and to Canada.
- **Ruby Lane:** its international policy could not be verified.

For a CNY 2,000 budget, these are mainly reference and price-ceiling sources, with occasional sub-USD 250 finds (mostly via Ruby Lane dealers, unverified).

### Cited Findings
- **1stDibs "Boucher enamel brooch" page.** Average about USD 5,750, range about USD 575–17,500. Listed pieces include Marcel Boucher enamel shrimp, radish, phoenix (1941) and peapod brooches. — [1stDibs: Boucher enamel brooch](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/boucher-enamel-brooch/)
- **1stDibs "Boucher brooch vintage costume jewellery."** Prices "start at $103," top out at USD 17,500, and average USD 1,479. 1980s Boucher Jewelers enamel + rhinestone pieces run USD 379–950 (average USD 575). — [1stDibs: boucher brooch vintage costume jewellery](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/boucher-brooch-vintage-costume-jewellery) (search snippet)
- **1stDibs duties.** Buyers outside a customs union owe inbound duties and taxes. Duties are not reimbursable by 1stDibs, and customs delays are not eligible for shipping refunds. — [1stDibs Support: Paying import duties](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/14491601612827-Paying-Import-Duties-Tariffs-and-Customs-Fees-on-International-Orders)
- **Chairish shipping.** Chairish facilitates shipping within the US and from the US to Canada, and its managed shipping is limited to those countries. Buyers elsewhere would need a forwarder, and duties and tariffs are the buyer's responsibility. — [Chairish: How Shipping Works](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/115007672667-How-Shipping-Works); [Chairish: Tariffs](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44253012844945-Tariffs)
- **Chairish example listing.** A signed Boucher clear-rhinestone brooch exists; the price was not captured. — [Chairish product 24916376](https://www.chairish.com/product/24916376/vintage-signed-boucher-clear-rhinestone-brooch)
- **Ruby Lane.** No first-party international policy was found. Forwarder guides say Ruby Lane purchases "may not" ship to every country, and buyers use US forwarders. — [Borderoo: Ruby Lane international shipping](https://borderoo.com/pages/buy-from-ruby-lanes-usa-online-store-international-shipping/)

### Inferences
- 1stDibs is too expensive for this budget except for rare lower-end pieces. It is still useful for learning which models are "top tier" (Boucher figurals, Trifari sterling).
- Ruby Lane is a dealer-run vintage mall. Shipping is decided by each shop, so a buyer in China should expect to ask the seller or use a US forwarder (inference).

### Gaps
- Ruby Lane search returned 503, so I have no current price samples or policy text.
- Whether 1stDibs ships jewelry directly to mainland China was not confirmed.
- 1stDibs buyer-protection terms for international buyers were not retrieved.

## Auction platforms: Catawiki, LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable, the-saleroom, Proxibid; buyer's premiums; lot prices

### Takeaway
- **Catawiki** has the clearest and cheapest fee: a 9% + €3 buyer protection fee, with shipping paid through the platform. It allows mainland-China bidders (with category restrictions), but each lot page states whether the seller ships to your country.
- **LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable and the-saleroom** are aggregators. The house's own buyer's premium, typically about 20–28%, plus a platform surcharge of about 3–6% pushes total fees toward 25–35%. Many small houses also do not ship abroad and require third-party packers, which makes them poor value for sub-USD 200 brooch lots.

### Cited Findings
- **Catawiki fee.** The buyer protection fee is 9% of the final bid plus €3; for example, a €100 bid gives a €12 fee. The fee is non-refundable, and shipping must be paid through Catawiki. — [Catawiki: What is the buyer protection fee](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/fees/what-is-the-buyer-protection-fee); [Catawiki: auction fees](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/fees/what-are-the-auction-fees)
- **Catawiki shipping and bidding restrictions.** "Some sellers may not be able to ship to your location," and this is shown on the object page. Bidders in "the US, Canada, Japan, Taiwan, mainland China, and Singapore" are restricted from some categories (alcohol, rugs, militaria), which implies mainland-China bidders can otherwise take part. Delivery outside the EU generally takes 10–20 working days, and customs fees may apply. — [Catawiki: Can objects be shipped to any country](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/buyer-shipping-basics/can-objects-be-shipped-to-any-country); [Catawiki: Why can't I bid in certain auctions](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/bidding-issues/why-can-t-i-bid-in-certain-auctions)
- **LiveAuctioneers premiums.**
  - A guide reports the platform adds about 2–3% on top of house premiums of 20–26%+.
  - One house's terms show 23% for direct payment, 26% via Bidsquare and 28% via LiveAuctioneers.
  - Jeffrey S. Evans charges a 27.5% buyer's premium on lots won via Bidsquare or LiveAuctioneers, plus processing fees.
  - An older Kovels article cited a 5–10% internet surcharge (likely outdated).
  — [Invaluable lot terms example](https://www.invaluable.com/auction-lot/two-inuit-eskimo-figural-carvings-including-maggi-324-c-8e9451bb89); [Jeffrey S. Evans buyer's premium](https://jeffreysevans.com/buying/buyers-premium); [Kovels](https://kovels.com/?p=2307658)
- **the-saleroom surcharges (set by each house):**
  - Plymouth Auction Rooms (2025): +6% including VAT.
  - Gildings: 5.94% including VAT.
  - Bellmans: +5% on top of the buyer's premium.
  - Bonhams: 4% third-party platform fee.
  — [Plymouth Auctions](https://plymouthauctions.co.uk/?p=2432); [Gildings: changes to live bidding fees](https://gildings.co.uk/news-item/update-to-terms-changes-to-live-bidding-fees); [Bellmans FAQ](https://bellmans.co.uk/faqs); [Bonhams auction terms](https://www.bonhams.com/auction/32207/lot/30/dia-al-azzawi-iraq-born-1939-untitled-525cm-x-36cm-painted-in-1981)
- **Proxibid.** Buyer's premium is set by each auctioneer. Only a help-center link surfaced. — [Proxibid auctionbuilder help](https://auctionbuilder.proxibid.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4417501015313)

### Inferences
- **Worked fee example.** A USD 100 hammer on LiveAuctioneers at a 28% premium costs USD 128 before shipping. Add USD 30–60 for a packer or courier and any per-lot fees, and auction houses are poor value for single low-value brooches.
- **Mixed lots.** Mixed costume-jewelry lots ("signed brooches x 10") can be good value per piece, but authentication is weak and shipping is harder.
- **Catawiki's 9% + €3 is the lowest total fee** among the auction options. Its curated weekly "Costume jewellery" auctions are worth watching, but confirm on each lot that it ships to China.

### Gaps
- No Catawiki, LiveAuctioneers or the-saleroom hammer prices for named-maker costume brooches were retrieved.
- Invaluable's own platform surcharge was not verified.
- Whether Catawiki seller shipping to China is common for jewelry lots was not verified.

## European resale: Vinted, Vestiaire Collective, Leboncoin, Kleinanzeigen

### Takeaway
- **Vestiaire Collective** lists "China" (separately from Hong Kong) among its 70 operating countries, so it is the one European resale app where a China-address buyer appears to be supported directly. Its authentication service for costume jewelry was not verified.
- **Vinted** works only inside regional corridors; none documented includes China.
- **Leboncoin and Kleinanzeigen** are essentially domestic and impractical without a local intermediary.

### Cited Findings
- **Vestiaire countries.** Vestiaire operates for shipping and billing addresses in 70 countries, including "China" and "Hong Kong" as separate entries; Taiwan is also listed. If a country is not listed, users cannot buy or sell. — [Vestiaire FAQ: In which countries does Vestiaire Collective operate](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4426475347729-In-which-countries-does-Vestiaire-Collective-operate)
- **Vestiaire shipping fees.** A "Shipping Fees – Rest of the World" FAQ exists (contents not retrieved). A third-party forwarder (ColisExpat) advertises delivery of Vestiaire purchases to China. — [Vestiaire: Shipping Fees Rest of the World](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4411762037521-Shipping-Fees-Rest-of-the-World); [ColisExpat](https://www.colisexpat.com/en/delivery-shipping/china/vestiaire-collective/)
- **Vinted corridors.** Cross-border trade is limited: UK↔US, UK↔Australia (2025 launch), and regional EU pools. Buyers "assume all risks associated with international shipping and importation." — [Vinted Help 1555](https://www.vinted.com/help/1555); [Value Added Resource: Vinted Australia](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/vinted-quietly-launches-australia/); [Vinted terms](https://vinted.com/terms-and-conditions)
- **Vinted via forwarders.** Forwarder guides describe buying from outside Europe by using a European forwarding address. — [FrenchForwarders: buying on Vinted from abroad](https://frenchforwarders.com/buying-in-another-country-on-vinted-how-it-works/)

### Inferences
- **Vestiaire inventory.** Vestiaire stock leans toward "designer" costume pieces (Dior, YSL, Givenchy, Chanel), usually above USD 150. A YSL or Givenchy brooch under CNY 2,000 landed is possible but uncommon (inference; no price sample).
- **Vinted and French/German classifieds.** These hold underpriced stock from non-specialist sellers, which is good for Dior/Grosse and Butler & Wilson. Reaching it requires a forwarder address in that country, and Vinted protection covers delivery to the forwarder only.

### Gaps
- Whether Vestiaire's authentication applies to costume (non-precious) jewelry, and its fee level for China buyers, was not verified.
- Leboncoin and Kleinanzeigen buyer-protection and shipping rules for foreign buyers were not researched (search budget exhausted).
- No Vestiaire or Vinted brooch price samples were collected.

### Takeaway
I found no current, verifiable first-weight (首重) price list for Chinese 转运 companies. Forwarders are mainly needed for Etsy, Ruby Lane, Chairish, Vinted and auction houses that will not ship to China. On eBay, EIS removes the need.

### Cited Findings
- **Only dated pricing found.** The only Chinese-language result was a 2019 smzdm promotion ("转运首重包邮"), which is expired. — [smzdm 2019 article](https://www.smzdm.com/p/12874651/)

- **Western forwarders serving China** include Borderoo and MyUS (for Ruby Lane and US stores), ColisExpat (France/Vestiaire) and FrenchForwarders (Vinted). — [Borderoo](https://borderoo.com/pages/buy-from-ruby-lanes-usa-online-store-international-shipping/); [ColisExpat](https://www.colisexpat.com/en/delivery-shipping/china/vestiaire-collective/); [FrenchForwarders](https://frenchforwarders.com/buying-in-another-country-on-vinted-how-it-works/)

### Inferences
- A brooch parcel is usually under 200 g. Chinese 转运 lines commonly price by first 0.5 kg; typical market rates were not verified here. Budget about CNY 100–250 for the forwarder leg and confirm on the forwarder's site.

## Best value-for-money platform recommendations

### Takeaway
1. **eBay US sellers offering EIS to China** are the first choice: deepest inventory, transparent sold-price data, and the Money Back Guarantee extended to international buyers.
2. **Catawiki** is second, with the lowest auction fee (9% + €3) and curated costume-jewelry auctions, as long as the lot page confirms China shipping.
3. **Etsy** is third, for browsing and for sellers that ship to China directly.
4. **Vestiaire** is fourth, for French designer pieces (YSL, Givenchy, Dior), since it lists China as a supported country.
5. **1stDibs and Chairish** are for reference only. **LiveAuctioneers, Invaluable and the-saleroom** are uneconomic for single brooches under about USD 200 once a 25–35% fee load and shipping are added.

### Cited Findings
- eBay EIS includes China — [eBay Help 5348](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348). The MBG covers international purchases — [eBay Help 4057](https://www.ebay.com/help/buying/shipping-delivery/changing-delivery-address-method/international-purchases-shipping?id=4057).
- HDB excludes second-hand, auction and Best Offer items, and ends MBG coverage — [eBay HDB](https://www.ebay.com/cdp/help/freight/hdb).
- Catawiki fee is 9% + €3 — [Catawiki fee help](https://auction.catawiki.com/en/help/fees/what-is-the-buyer-protection-fee).
- LiveAuctioneers / the-saleroom premiums plus surcharges are about 25–34% — [Invaluable lot terms](https://www.invaluable.com/auction-lot/two-inuit-eskimo-figural-carvings-including-maggi-324-c-8e9451bb89); [Gildings](https://gildings.co.uk/news-item/update-to-terms-changes-to-live-bidding-fees).
- 1stDibs Boucher enamel brooch average is about USD 5,750 — [1stDibs](https://www.1stdibs.com/buy/boucher-enamel-brooch/).
- Chairish ships only to the US and Canada — [Chairish](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/115007672667-How-Shipping-Works).
- Vestiaire lists China — [Vestiaire FAQ](https://faq.vestiairecollective.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4426475347729-In-which-countries-does-Vestiaire-Collective-operate).

### Inferences
- **Budget bands (CNY, landed):**
  - About CNY 400–900: Trifari, Coro, Coro Craft, BSK-tier enamel + rhinestone pieces (item about USD 40–100).
  - About CNY 900–1,600: good Boucher figurals, Hattie Carnegie, and smaller sterling Coro Craft or Trifari (item about USD 100–180).
  - Near or over the limit: Mazer or Trifari sterling "jelly bellies," large Boucher, early Eisenberg sterling, signed Dior/Grosse.
  - These bands come from the sourced USD figures plus estimated USD 25–45 shipping and import charges; treat them as estimates.
- **Authenticity practice on any platform:**
  - Ask for photos of the mark (e.g., "Boucher" with an inventory number, "Trifari" with crown, "Coro Craft Sterling" with the Pegasus, "Chr. Dior Germany" with the year).
  - Check the back for replaced stones and repaired enamel.
  - Prefer sellers who accept returns. eBay's MBG "item not as described" route is the strongest recourse a China-based buyer has.

### Gaps
- No direct comparison of total landed cost on the same item across platforms was possible.
- Collector-forum experiences (Vintage Fashion Guild, Reddit r/vintagejewelry) and 小红书/知乎 海淘 guides were not retrieved within the tool budget.
