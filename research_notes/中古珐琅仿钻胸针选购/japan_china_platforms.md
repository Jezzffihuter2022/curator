# Japan and China channels for vintage enamel and rhinestone brooches (buyer in mainland China, landed budget CNY 2,000 or less)

Research date: 2026-11-23. Yahoo! Auctions (ヤフオク) closed-auction pages were fetched on this date. They show a 180-day window and give the end date as month/day with no year. Because the most recent end dates are 11/22–11/23, these are taken to be Oct–Nov 2026 sales, which is an inference. The per-listing tables were summarised by a fetch model from the live pages. They are accurate enough for price levels, but check individual listings before quoting them as exact.

**Exchange rate:** the CFETS central parity was 100 JPY = 4.1960 CNY on 16 Nov 2026 and 4.2283 on 5 Nov 2026 ([Xinhua 11-16](https://www.news.cn/20261116/52e2a21498fd477f9d032e1c54778272/c.html), [Xinhua 11-05](https://xinhuanet.com/20261105/7902f95153094f2c8134a28577aea4b7/c.html)). At about 4.20 CNY per 100 JPY, **CNY 2,000 is about ¥47,600**, and ¥10,000 is about CNY 420.

## Q1. Japan: marketplaces, price levels by brand, why Japan is cheap, search keywords

### Takeaway
Yahoo! Auctions sold data for Nov 2026 shows the following:
- **Unbranded or minor-brand vintage enamel and rhinestone brooches** usually sell for ¥500–5,000 (about CNY 20–210).
- **Trifari** pieces mostly sell for ¥1,000–8,000.
- **YSL and Dior vintage brooches** cluster at ¥5,000–25,000 (about CNY 210–1,050).
- **Chanel** has a median near ¥30,000 and an average of ¥38,000 (about CNY 1,250–1,600). Gripoix and pearl pieces reach ¥108,000, which is over budget.

Apart from most Chanel, everything on the target list fits inside the CNY 2,000 landed budget when bought in Japan.

**Why Japan is cheap**
- The yen hit a 38-year low against the USD in June 2024, which made Japanese secondhand fashion cheap for foreign buyers. Buyee reported growing purchases from the US and China ([nssmag](https://www.nssmag.com/en/fashion/37843/vintage-japan-valuation-yen)).
- JETRO (2026) says "Used in Japan" goods are valued abroad for good condition and trusted authenticity control ([JETRO](https://www.jetro.go.jp/biz/areareports/2026/44569f34646f064b.html)).
- Japan has a large secondhand market and many vintage shops ([Forbes Japan](https://forbesjapan.com/articles/detail/79121)).

### Inferences
- **Why Japan prices are low:** I found no source specific to costume jewellery for this. The likely reasons are a combination of factors:
  1. The weak yen. At about 4.2 CNY/100 JPY, a Dior brooch selling for ¥8,500 is about CNY 360.
  2. A large domestic supply. Japan imported Western brand costume jewellery heavily in the 1970s–90s bubble era, and estate clearances now feed ¥1-start auctions.
  3. Many sellers are general reuse or estate dealers, not jewellery specialists. They list with minimal descriptions, such as "ゴールドカラー" (gold colour), so a brooch that is enamel plus rhinestone plus a signature is often not tagged with those keywords. That depresses visibility and price.
- **Budget fit, before overhead:**
  - Trifari, Coro, Monet and unsigned pieces: almost always ¥500–8,000.
  - Dior and YSL: usually ¥5,000–25,000.
  - Givenchy: no closed search was run, so this is unknown (see Gaps).
  - Chanel: an enamel or rhinestone Chanel under about ¥35,000 hammer is possible but is a minority of sales. Gripoix pieces exceed budget.
- **Keywords that worked on Yahoo:**
  - ヴィンテージ ブローチ エナメル ラインストーン
  - トリファリ ブローチ / クラウントリファリ
  - ディオール ブローチ ヴィンテージ
  - イヴサンローラン ブローチ ヴィンテージ (also try "YSL ブローチ")
  - シャネル ブローチ ヴィンテージ
- **Further keywords to try:**
  - Boucher: マルセルブーシェ / ブーシェ ブローチ / BOUCHER ブローチ. "ボウシェ" returned no exact matches.
  - Givenchy: ジバンシー ブローチ / ジバンシィ
  - Coro: コロ ブローチ / CORO
  - Eisenberg: アイゼンバーグ
  - Hattie Carnegie: ハッティカーネギー
  - Mazer: メイザー
  - Butler & Wilson: バトラー＆ウィルソン
  - Swarovski: スワロフスキー ブローチ ヴィンテージ
  - Sterling: スターリング / SILVER925 / ヴェルメイユ
  - Other descriptors: 七宝 (enamel/cloisonné), 刻印あり (signed), 50年代 / 60年代
- **Japanese brand-buyout chains** such as Komehyo, Brand Off, RAGTAG and 2nd Street price above the auction market. The G-Rare evidence shows Dior rhinestone brooches at ¥39,800–49,800. Their advantage is in-house authentication. I did not individually verify their brooch prices or return policies (see Gaps).

## Q2. Proxy and forwarding services for China-based buyers, plus Japan to China shipping

### Takeaway
- **Buyee** is the best-documented option. It charges a ¥500 purchase fee per order, has optional plans up to ¥500, and accepts Alipay in CNY and UnionPay.
- **ZenMarket** charges ¥300–800 per item, with ¥800 for Mercari.
- **Japan Post to China is cheap:** a brooch parcel costs about ¥750 by small packet airmail or ¥1,450 by EMS at 500g. Rates rise on 1 Feb 2027.
- **Chinese-language 日淘 apps** (任你购GO, 日淘市集, 任空间, 挖煤姬) exist. I could not verify their fees, or that 中淘 or 99日淘 exist.

### Cited Findings

**Buyee**
- Purchase fee: ¥500 per order, paid with the first payment ([Buyee fee page](https://bc.help.buyee.jp/en/fee/)).
- Guarantee plan: up to ¥500.
- Domestic and international shipping are charged at actual cost. Package consolidation, protective packaging and photo service are optional paid extras; amounts are not stated on the fee page.
- Payment: Alipay (CNY only), UnionPay, Visa, Mastercard, JCB, PayPal and AFTEE. PayPal accounts registered in China can pay only in USD.
- Exchange rate: the market rate between 11:00 and 12:00 JST ([Buyee fee page](https://bc.help.buyee.jp/en/fee/)).
- Plans: Standard is free, Inspection Plan is ¥300 and Insured Delivery is ¥500. International shipping and protective packaging fees are non-refundable, and customs duties are not included ([Buyee plans](https://bc.help.buyee.jp/en/plans)).
- A third-party site says the fee rose from ¥300 to ¥500 on 1 Apr 2026 ([japanlanded](https://japanlanded.pages.dev/articles/buyee-vs-zenmarket/)). That is consistent with the current official ¥500, but the date is not confirmed on an official page.

**ZenMarket**
- The official fees page says ¥300–800 per item: ¥500 standard, ¥300 for recommended stores, and ¥800 for all Mercari items and JDirectItems Auction ([ZenMarket fees](https://zenmarket.jp/en/fees.aspx)).
- An older ZenMarket page shows a flat ¥500 ([zenmarket.jp/fees.aspx](https://www.zenmarket.jp/fees.aspx)).
- A direct fetch returned 403, so these figures come from search snippets.

**FromJapan and Neokyo**
- A third-party aggregator says FromJapan is ¥500 per item flat (from Jul 2024). It says Neokyo is ¥350 per item plus a ¥500 packaging fee per shipment ([japanlanded calculator](https://japanlanded.pages.dev/tools/japan-proxy-fee-calculator/)).
- Neither is confirmed on an official page.

**Chinese-language services**
- **任你购GO (极速日淘):** an iOS app that proxies Mercari (煤炉), Amazon JP, Yahoo Auctions, Suruga-ya, Rakuten and other sites. It offers international direct mail, including tax-included (包税) lines ([App Store CN](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E4%BB%BB%E4%BD%A0%E8%B4%ADgo-%E6%9E%81%E9%80%9F%E6%97%A5%E6%B7%98/id1559870015)).
- **日淘市集:** run by Inagora; covers Amazon JP, Mercari and Rakuten, with EMS, sea and small-packet lines ([App Store CN](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/%E6%97%A5%E6%B7%98%E5%B8%82%E9%9B%86-%E5%8F%A3%E8%A2%8B%E9%87%8C%E7%9A%84%E8%B6%A3%E5%91%B3%E6%97%A5%E6%9C%AC/id1636622724)).
- **任空间:** claims to be an official Mercari partner and accepts Alipay. **挖煤姬:** user reports say it supports Alipay and WeChat and offers 90 days of free storage. Both come from search-result summaries of [smzdm](https://post.smzdm.com/p/a03067xr/) and [bilibili](https://www.bilibili.com/opus/471958185044336354) posts, and are unverified.

**Japan Post to China (Zone 1, which covers China, Korea and Taiwan)**
- EMS: ¥1,450 up to 500g and ¥2,200 up to 1kg, on the rate schedule as of 1 Jan 2026 ([Japan Post EMS Zone 1](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/list-ems/zone1_en.html)).
- Small Packet airmail (小形包装物): ¥350 up to 100g, ¥450 up to 200g, ¥750 up to 500g, ¥1,250 up to 1kg and ¥2,250 up to 2kg ([Japan Post letter-post Zone 1](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/send/oversea/charge/list-normal/zone1_en.html)).
- **1 Feb 2027 rate revision** (announced 9 Oct 2026): EMS, international parcels and letter-post including small packets all rise. Zone 1 EMS becomes ¥2,850 for 1kg, and the special surcharge (特別追加料金) is set to ¥0 ([Japan Post notice](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/send/oversea/information/fee_revision_2027/); [Netshop Impress](https://netshop.impress.co.jp/n/2026/10/15/16866)).
- Japan Post's 2025 suspension of goods shipments applied to the **USA** (end of de minimis), not China ([Japan Post press release 2025-08](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/notification/pressrelease/2025/00_honsha/0825_01_01.pdf)).

### Inferences
- **Worked landed-cost example** for one Dior brooch hammered at ¥10,000 via Buyee (shipping weight about 100–200g):
  - Item ¥10,000 + Buyee ¥500 + Japanese domestic shipping about ¥200–500 (assumed) + EMS ¥1,450 = about ¥12,200, or **about CNY 510** before Chinese tax.
  - Consolidation, protective packing and Buyee's own international-shipping markups may add some hundreds of yen. Buyee's shipping method fees were not verified.
- **Shipping choice:** for a single brooch, small packet airmail (about ¥450–750) is the cheapest option. EMS (¥1,450) adds tracking, compensation and speed. Proxies' own carrier options differ.
- **Ship before 1 Feb 2027** if possible, because Japan Post rates rise then.
- **Budget ceiling:** at CNY 2,000 total, the maximum hammer price is roughly ¥38,000–42,000. This assumes about ¥2,500 of overhead and about 20% Chinese tax if assessed; see Q3.

### Gaps
- Buyee's international shipping method list and handling surcharges for China, and consolidation and packing fees: not retrieved.
- 中淘 and 99日淘: no reliable information found, so their existence and fees are unverified. 任你购, 日淘市集, 任空间 and 挖煤姬 fees: not verified.
- New 2027 small packet and 500g EMS rates for China: the PDF could not be parsed.

## Q3. China-side channels (闲鱼, 小红书, 得物, 淘宝/天猫 中古店, 微拍堂, 红布林, 只二, HK/TW): prices, authentication, returns, import tax

### Takeaway
I could not retrieve China-side listing prices; 闲鱼 and 小红书 are app-only or login-walled. Platform authentication evidence is weak for costume jewellery:
- **闲鱼's luxury consignment (保真奢品)** covers bags, watches and 首饰 (jewellery). I found no confirmation that 验货宝 covers vintage costume brooches.
- **红布林** advertises multi-step authentication and a "假一赔三" (pay 3x if fake) guarantee, but has many consumer complaints. I found no brooch-specific policy.

Counterfeit risk for Dior and Chanel costume pieces is high on the China side. Signatures and stampings are the main check, and older Dior lacks reference documentation.

### Cited Findings
- **闲鱼:**
  - In Feb 2026 闲鱼 upgraded its "保真奢品" business with full-process consignment custody covering bags, watches and 首饰 ([Sina](https://sina.cn/detail/nikrvzn9381504)).
  - Third-party articles describe 验货宝 as buyer opt-in at checkout (item price plus inspection fee), with fees quoted inconsistently: ¥39–79, ¥19–50, or ¥100–200 for bags. No article lists jewellery or brooches.
  - One article warns that for small jewellery under CNY 2,000 the authentication fee may not be worth it ([Sina search results](https://www.sina.cn/news/article/niizkxi1343049.html)). These are commercial explainer articles; treat with caution.
- **红布林 (Plum):**
  - Claims a four-step authentication process and "假一赔三" insurance with 泰康保险 (Taikang Insurance).
  - Has a strategic cooperation with CCIC's luxury authentication centre.
  - Media say consignment items support 7-day no-reason returns ([Sina](https://www.sina.cn/news/article/nikufhp0678401.html); [PingWest](https://www.pingwest.com/a/317769)).
  - Many 黑猫投诉 (Black Cat complaints) about after-sales service, authenticity disputes and pricing ([tousu.sina](https://tousu.sina.com.cn/articles/view/380693)).
  - No brooch or costume jewellery policy was found.
- **Authentication advice in Chinese sources:**
  - Use CCIC or national-inspection certification, and avoid individual sellers.
  - Check stamping font, depth and crispness. Fake Chanel stampings are often shallow, rounded or rippled.
  - Dior's anti-counterfeit system is less complete than Chanel's, and old Dior items lack cards or chips, which makes them harder to authenticate.
  - Vintage jewellery pitfalls include material substitution, over-restoration and misdescribed condition.
  - All from commercial explainer articles on [Sina](https://www.sina.cn/news/article/nimffsr5285152.html).
- **Chinese collector commentary:** Trifari has the largest surviving supply of any vintage brand and suits beginners. Except collection and designer lines, 1950s–60s Trifari prices have not risen noticeably. Brand premiums have risen in recent years and online buying is risky ([Dealmoon post](https://www.dealmoon.com/post/1469485)).
- **US price comparison** (eBay, USD, excluding shipping): a Crown Trifari enamel and rhinestone brooch at about US$105, an enamel and faux-pearl duck at about US$80, and a basic enamel and crystal bar at about US$27 ([Dealmoon/eBay search summary](https://www.dealmoon.com/post/1469485)). Exact listing-level figures were not verified.
- **China import tax on personal parcels:**
  - Under the Classification Table of Imported Items, effective 1 Dec 2024, jewellery rates are: precious metal (gold, silver, platinum) jewellery 13%, diamonds 20%, non-diamond precious jewellery and gems 50%, and other jewellery 20%.
  - If the total tax is CNY 50 or less, it is exempt.
  - A multi-item shipment must not exceed CNY 2,000; a single indivisible item may exceed this ([Spaceship](https://support.spaceshipapp.com/en/china-personal-import-tax-rates)).
  - The personal-goods declaration threshold rose from CNY 1,000 to CNY 2,000 from 1 Dec 2024 under GACC Announcement 2024 No. 176 ([DHL](https://www.dhl.com/discover/zh-tw/ship-with-dhl/export-with-dhl/china-regulatory-changes)).

### Inferences
- **Tax treatment:** gold-plated base-metal costume brooches likely fall under "other jewellery" at 20%. Sterling or vermeil may qualify for the 13% precious-metal line. This is inference from the table structure; confirm against the official GACC table.
- **When tax applies:** tax at 20% of more than about CNY 250 dutiable value exceeds the CNY 50 exemption. Customs assesses on its own 完税价格 (dutiable value) and inspects postal parcels selectively, so tax is possible but not certain. Budget about 20% on top for safety.
- **Splitting parcels:** keep each parcel's declared value well under CNY 2,000, and ship separately rather than consolidating many items.
- **China-side prices:** domestic 中古 dealers on 闲鱼, 小红书 and 淘宝 typically source from Japan, so their prices should sit above Japanese auction prices plus overhead. No listing data was retrieved to quantify the markup, so this is inference.
- **Authentication coverage:** for brooches under CNY 2,000, platform authentication is either unavailable or uneconomic. Buyer protection rests mainly on the platform's general dispute process and the seller's reputation.

### Gaps
- No current 闲鱼, 小红书, 得物, 淘宝, 微拍堂, 只二 or HK/TW dealer prices were retrieved; the platforms are app-only or login-walled.
- No confirmation of 闲鱼 验货宝 or 得物 coverage for costume brooches.
- No official 红布林 return policy for jewellery.
- 得物: not researched. 只二 and 微拍堂: no data.

## Q4. Price comparison and recommendations, with red flags

### Takeaway
Japan via a proxy, mainly Yahoo! Auctions with Mercari as a secondary option, gives the best value for this budget:
- **A signed Dior, YSL or Trifari enamel and rhinestone brooch** can usually be landed for CNY 100–1,200, including Japan Post and plausible 20% tax.
- **Chanel** is borderline but feasible below about ¥35,000 hammer.
- **China-side channels** were not price-sampled, so a quantified Japan vs. China comparison is not possible from this research.

### Inferences
- **Recommended channel order:**
  1. **Yahoo! Auctions via Buyee** or a Chinese 日淘 proxy. It has the deepest sold-price transparency and many ¥1-start estate listings. Buyee takes Alipay and UnionPay; add its Inspection Plan (¥300) for Chanel or Dior.
  2. **Mercari JP via a proxy**, for fixed prices. Note the higher ZenMarket ¥800 per-item fee for Mercari.
  3. **Japanese used-brand chains** (Komehyo, Brand Off, RAGTAG) only for Chanel, where in-house authentication matters. Expect retail premiums.
  4. **China-side 闲鱼 and 小红书**, only from established 中古 dealers who show stamping close-ups and accept returns. Avoid Chanel or Dior "vintage" offered by individuals at below-Japan prices.
- **Value sweet spot:** signed American costume makers (Trifari, Coro, Monet, Boucher, Hattie Carnegie) bought in Japan give the most enamel and rhinestone quality per yuan. Trifari sterling from the 1940s, often "Sterling" with crown-T marks, is the vermeil target. Use the search terms "スターリング" or "STERLING" with "トリファリ".
- **Red flags:**
  - Missing or blurry signature photos, or stampings that look cast or shallow.
  - "Vintage button" Chanel items, which are converted buttons and not original brooches.
  - Lots described only by colour ("ゴールドカラー"), unless priced as junk.
  - ノークレーム・ノーリターン (no claims, no returns) on high-value Chanel or Dior.
  - Chinese sellers who refuse close-up photos.
  - Prices far below the medians above for Chanel or Dior.
  - Rhinestones described as "石取れ" (missing stones) or enamel loss (エナメル剥げ). Repairing either costs more than the price difference.
- **Tax planning:** ship one or two brooches per parcel, keep each parcel's declared value modest, and stay under CNY 2,000 per parcel to avoid the multi-item limit issue.

### Gaps
- No same-item, same-date price pairs between Japan and China channels; China-side data was not retrievable.
- No price data for Givenchy, Boucher, Coro, Hattie Carnegie, Mazer, Eisenberg, Sphinx, Butler & Wilson or vintage Swarovski in Japan. These need follow-up searches with the keywords listed in Q1.
